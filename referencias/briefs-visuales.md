Las tres imágenes de concepto se generaron con la herramienta integrada ImageGen. El plano 04 se dibujó a partir de la línea central del prompt maestro.

## 01-carrera.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate one landscape 16:9 AAA indie 3D videogame keyframe as an honest example of the MAIN SHARED GAMEPLAY CAMERA for a tiny toy-car grand prix on a workbench. This is a fixed, high three-quarter camera, looking down roughly 50 degrees, with a gentle wide lens and deep focus. Show the entire continuous closed paved circuit in one readable screen, nearly edge to edge except a dedicated bottom-right corner, with the finish straight running left to right across the lower third. The motorsport geometry evokes a professional motorcycle grand prix road course: smooth dark graphite tarmac with one forward route, exact red/ivory kerbs, racing-green painted runoffs, a rounded hairpin on the right, a raised shallow left-right S across the rear on tiny timber supports, a fast sweeper on the left, painted start-grid and checker stripe on the front straight. Plausible continuous asphalt with no crossovers and no junctions. Four small palm-sized die-cast toy race cars ONLY, one cyan compact hatch, one warm vermilion rally coupe, one lemon yellow rounded racer, one violet wedge racer, spread out and large enough to read, all with squat broad stance, black rubber tires and simple stripes. The very small scale is clear because the circuit is mounted on birch plywood with exposed lamination and proper little fences, and an oversized brushed steel wrench and red steel tool chest sit in the softly lit real workshop beyond the track. Amber practical light and cool graphite shadows, crisp warm miniature automotive model-kit textures, clean miniature stadium illumination, realistic materials in a crafted stylized 3D render. On the front-right tabletop shoulder, a small camera-facing unmarked matte ivory square recessed in a dark industrial holder, fully inside the bottom-right corner with space around it. This is deliberately a completely blank square intended for a later live QR. No actual QR, finder shapes, readable letters, UI, numbers over cars, overlays, banners, watermarks, bikes, people or phones. Keep every inch of racing road readable, strong geometric composition, real toy proportions. A single image, no contact sheet.

## 02-detalle.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate a single cinematic wide 16:9 3D material-reference render for MICRO GP, a tiny high-end handmade grand prix circuit for palm-sized die-cast toy cars mounted on a birch workshop table. This is a low trackside reference shot at the apex, from near the curb, not a gameplay camera. A cyan boxy compact racing hatch with a simple ivory center stripe leads a vermilion rally coupe around a fast bend. Four black rubber wheels per car, glossy painted die-cast bodies, substantial toy silhouettes and tactile tiny screws. The tarmac is fine graphite, the painted kerb is alternating red and off-white, and the dark racing-green painted run-off follows the curve. Miniature tire stacks, scale safety fence and plywood laminations. An oversized silver spanner and an old red steel tool chest out of focus at the back establish toy scale. Warm tungsten workshop practicals, some cool fill, rich realistic materials, controlled depth of field, crisp readable leading car and curb, no exaggerated motion effects. Composition has a strong diagonal racing line, late-afternoon amber light with deep slate shadows, with the same color/material choices as a handcrafted premium slot-car-style diorama. Keep the road free of slots and rails. No people, no motorcycles, no overlays, no QR, no labels, no typography, no visible logos or watermarks. This should look like a beautifully lit realtime 3D game environment material study.

## 03-mando.png

Use case: ui-mockup. Generate a single landscape 16:9, highly legible straight-on product render of a phone held horizontally as a mobile browser arcade controller. Place the entire single phone centered, filling roughly 90% of image width, on a dark graphite seamless background with warm reflected edge lighting. Front orthographic product view, phone glass screen square to camera with absolutely no perspective distortion. Nothing else in the image, no hands. Inside the phone, one complete retro arcade-control panel fills the usable display like a physical object: subtly pebbled charcoal arcade plastic, thin industrial cream pinstripe, a few real corner screws. LEFT HALF: exactly one large tactile arcade joystick with a black rubber boot in a circular recessed gate and a warm ivory spherical knob, illustrated like a polished 3D object, enough room around it for a thumb. RIGHT HALF: exactly two generously separated circular physical arcade push buttons, horizontally side by side at the same height. The inward button is vermilion red and says "FRENO" exactly in bold dark high contrast lettering centered on its face. The outer rightmost button is vivid cyan and says "GAS" exactly in bold dark high contrast lettering centered on its face. The text must read FRENO, GAS, no other visible letters or marks. A single tiny cyan pilot light near the top center echoes the player's toy-car paint, and looks like a light rather than a control. No extra buttons, d-pad, second stick, arrows, menus, text outside buttons, status bar, touch outlines, racing game preview, QR, captions or watermark. Dark premium industrial design, ivory hardware, warm edges, clean deliberate spacing. This is a functional mobile thumb placement mockup, not a standalone physical gamepad. Show the smartphone edges clearly, and keep the interface readable at thumbnail size.
